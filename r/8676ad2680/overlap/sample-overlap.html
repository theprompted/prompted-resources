The Overlap

One design. Three ads.

The same nurse design, turned into three completely different ads. They're different not because the generator got creative, but because each one sits in the overlap of three things: who she is, what she wants, and a design that's easy for AI to render cleanly. Each ad below speaks to a different reason people buy a shirt. Same design held exact via a reference image. One prompt each.

Want 1 · Be seen wearing it

Leaving the hospital at sunrise.

The "what": She wants to be seen wearing it in a circumstance that says who she is. Walking out after a night shift, coffee in hand, the shirt makes the claim public, this is me, and I survived another one. The setting (the hospital doors, the staff entrance) does the identity work.

The prompt
A nurse in her late 30s walking out of the hospital doors at sunrise after a night shift, a coffee cup in one hand held low at her hip, wearing the cream tee under an open jacket, golden morning light, a quiet confident half-smile, arms relaxed so the chest is clear. The shirt MUST use the EXACT design from the attached reference image, same retro groovy "THIS NURSE IS STRONGER THAN HER THIRD COFFEE" artwork, same warm mustard/orange/brown colors, same daisy and coffee-cup elements, on a cream/natural t-shirt. The design must be LARGE, occupying at least 55-60% of the visible chest, fully legible, sharp focus, facing the camera, ONLY on the front. Real photographic ad. Even flattering light on the chest so the text reads clearly.
Want 2 · Feel something

Mid-laugh at her own shirt.

The "what": She just wants to feel a certain thing, here, the humor, the in-on-the-joke pride. The ad sells the feeling, not the setting: tight framing, a real laugh, the mug to the side. She sees herself in that moment and wants to feel it too.

The prompt
Tight confident portrait of a nurse mid-laugh at the joke on her own shirt, head tilted with genuine joy, holding a coffee mug to the side at hip level so the chest is fully clear, warm cozy kitchen light. The shirt MUST use the EXACT design from the attached reference image, same retro groovy "THIS NURSE IS STRONGER THAN HER THIRD COFFEE" artwork, same warm mustard/orange/brown colors, same daisy and coffee-cup elements, on a cream/natural t-shirt. The design must be LARGE, occupying at least 55-60% of the visible chest, fully legible, sharp focus, facing the camera, ONLY on the front. Real photographic ad. Even flattering light on the chest so the text reads clearly.
Want 3 · Gift it

Handing it to a coworker.

The "what": She wants to give it to someone who'd get it. In print on demand the buyer is often not the wearer, it's the daughter, the partner, the work friend. So the ad shows the giving moment: the shirt held open and facing the camera so you can read exactly what you'd be gifting. Sentimentality baked in beats any old t-shirt.

The prompt
A nurse handing a folded cream tee held open and facing the camera so the design reads clearly, to a beaming coworker in scrubs, warm break-room moment with coffee mugs on the counter behind them.
