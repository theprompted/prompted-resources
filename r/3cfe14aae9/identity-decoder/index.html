01Get started in three steps

You need Claude Code already installed (you're a paid Claude subscriber). That's the only assumption.

  1. Download the zip using the button above. Move it to your Desktop and double-click to unzip. You'll get a folder called identity-decoder.
  2. Copy it into your skills folder. Move the identity-decoder folder into .claude/skills/ in your project (or your home folder's .claude/skills/ to make it available everywhere). If you're not sure, open Claude Code and ask it: "install the identity-decoder skill from this folder." cp -r ~/Desktop/identity-decoder ~/.claude/skills/
  3. Run it. Open Claude Code and start the skill. Give it a product URL and it does the rest: real research, the five scored archetypes, then the deep decode of the one you pick. /identity-decoder run identity decoder on [your-product-url]
⚠ Heads up — give it room to research

The scan does real web research across reviews, forums, and skeptic content before it generates the archetypes. That's what makes the output specific instead of generic. A full scan plus decode takes a few minutes. Let it run.

02What's in the skill

One skill, run as a three-step wizard:

Step 1 — Scan

Five scored buyer archetypes

Reads your product page, then researches reviews, forums, and skeptic content to surface the five distinct buyers in your market. Each is scored on market size and conversion ease, ranked by opportunity, so you know who's actually worth building for. Then it stops and asks which one to go deep on.

Step 2 — Decode (psychology)

The 10-section deep dive

For the archetype you pick: trigger moments, limiting beliefs, private shame, current workarounds, aspirational identity, hidden risks, tiny wins, moment of triumph, the identity contradiction, and the identity they're terrified of becoming. Raw creative ammunition, in their own words.

Step 3 — Decode (media + language)

What they consume, and how to talk to them

The images and videos they actually engage with (so your creative feels native), plus the vocabulary and scroll-stopping hooks that resonate. Everything written up as a clean HTML report.

03If something goes wrong

Just describe what you saw to Claude Code. It can read its own skill file, see where it stopped, and pick up from there.

  • Output feels generic — it usually means the research step got skipped or thin. Tell it to "redo the scan with real web research first" and name the sources (reviews, the product's subreddit, "is it worth it" videos).
  • It only produced a few psychology sections — tell it "finish all 10 sections." The full decode is the whole point.
  • The skill isn't found — confirm the identity-decoder folder is inside .claude/skills/ and restart Claude Code.
