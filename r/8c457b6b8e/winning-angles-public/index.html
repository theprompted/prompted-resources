01Get started in three steps

You need Claude Code already installed (you're a paid Claude subscriber). That's the only assumption.

  1. Download the zip using the button above. Move it to your Desktop and double-click to unzip. You'll get a folder called winning-angles-public containing three skills.
  2. Copy the skills into your project's .claude/skills/ folder, then open Claude Code there. In Terminal (Mac) or Command Prompt (Windows): cd your-project && claude The bundle holds find-angles (the front door), plus belief-mapper and contradiction-engine that it drives. Drop all three folders into .claude/skills/.
  3. Run the front door on your product URL. Once you see the ❯ prompt: /find-angles https://your-product-url.com It scans your market (5 scored buyer archetypes) and stops for you to pick one. Then it does the full decode + angle layers + the knife, and drops into a back-and-forth to land the angle you'd actually run.
↳ How it works

This is not a one-prompt magic button — and that's the design. It does the heavy lifting (research, decode, organized fodder, starter swings), then you connect the invisible dots to make the actual idea. That's the high-value part, done ~12–200× faster than before.

02What's in the bundle

Three composable skills that hand off to each other, plus their references:

find-angles — the front door

The orchestrator. One URL in, the winning angle out (via dialogue).

Takes your product URL and runs the whole machine: delegates the research, directs the setup, and dialogues the angle with you. Drives the other two skills. Trigger: /find-angles [url].

belief-mapper — the caddie read

Research → decode → tee up the angle.

Scans the market for 5 buyer archetypes (scored on size + conversion), then on the one you pick: the full 10-section psychological decode, the media diet, and the four angle layers — accepted beliefs, contradiction fuel, guardrails, and the Swiss Army knife spelled out.

contradiction-engine — the Swiss Army knife

Turn the belief map into angles you'd actually run.

Picks the belief that leaves your product standing, smart-selects which of the 6 contradiction blades fit (and skips the rest with reasons), proposes sharp angles, and loops with you to refine. One belief, many expressions. Includes the canonical blade definitions + verified examples.

Plus the shared output-template.html (consistent styling) and principle-texts.md (the decode's section principles).

03If something goes wrong

Just describe what you saw to Claude Code — it can read its own skill files and walk you through fixes.

  • The output feels thin or generic — the research probably came up short. The skills are built to fail loudly and name the gated sources rather than fabricate; if a key source (reviews, a forum) was blocked, feed it a direct link or a different angle and re-run.
  • The angles all feel the same — that means the blades were forced. The skill should smart-select only the blades that fit and skip the rest. Ask it to re-pick the belief, or change the posture (exposé vs. confidence).
  • It skipped the market scan — make sure you ran /find-angles (the front door), not one of the sub-skills directly.
